📱 iPhone Installation

1
Tap Share Button
Look for the share icon (box with arrow ↗️) at the bottom of Safari
2
Find "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu and tap this option
3
Tap "Add"
Confirm and the ARIOME icon will appear on your home screen!

🤖 Android Installation

1
Look for Install Banner
Chrome will show "Add ARIOME to Home screen" at the bottom
2
Or Use Menu
Tap menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
3
Tap "Install"
Confirm and the ARIOME icon appears instantly!
